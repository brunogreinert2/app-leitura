# Homeric Hymn 12: To Hera

**1** I sing of golden-throned Hera whom Rhea bare. Queen of the immortals is she, surpassing all in beauty: she is the sister and the wife of loud-thundering Zeus, —the glorious one whom all the blessed throughout high Olympus ^v-1

**5** reverence and honor even as Zeus who delights in thunder. ^v-5

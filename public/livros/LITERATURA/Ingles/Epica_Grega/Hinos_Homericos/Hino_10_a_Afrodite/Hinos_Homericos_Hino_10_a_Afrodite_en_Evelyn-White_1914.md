# Homeric Hymn 10: To Aphrodite

**1** Of Cytherea, born in Cyprus, I will sing. She gives kindly gifts to men: smiles are ever on her lovely face, and lovely is the brightness that plays over it. Hail, goddess, queen of well-built Salamis ^v-1

**5** and sea-girt Cyprus; grant me a cheerful song. And now I will remember you and another song also. ^v-5

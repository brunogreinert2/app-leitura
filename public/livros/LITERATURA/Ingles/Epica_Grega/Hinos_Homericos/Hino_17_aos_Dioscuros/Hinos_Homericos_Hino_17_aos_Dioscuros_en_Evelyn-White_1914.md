# Homeric Hymn 17: To the Dioscuri

**1** Sing, clear-voiced Muse, of Castor and Polydeuces, the Tyndaridae, who sprang from Olympian Zeus. Beneath the heights of Taygetus stately Leda bare them, when the dark-clouded Son of Cronos had privily bent her to his will. ^v-1

**5** Hail, children of Tyndareus, riders upon swift horses! ^v-5

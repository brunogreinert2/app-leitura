# Homeric Hymn 21: To Apollo

**1** Phoebus, of you even the swan sings with clear voice to the beating of his wings, as he alights upon the bank by the eddying river Peneus; and of you the sweet-tongued minstrel, holding his high-pitched lyre, always sings both first and last. ^v-1

**5** And so hail to you, lord! I seek your favor with my song. ^v-5

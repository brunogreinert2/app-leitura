# Homeric Hymn 24: To Hestia

**1** Hestia, you who tend the holy house of the lord Apollo, the Far-shooter at goodly Pytho, with soft oil dripping ever from your locks, come now into this house, come, having one mind ^v-1

**5** with Zeus the all-wise —draw near, and withal bestow grace upon my song. ^v-5

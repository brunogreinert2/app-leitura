# Homeric Hymn 16: To Asclepius

**1** I begin to sing of Asclepius, son of Apollo and healer of sicknesses. In the Dotian plain fair Coronis, daughter of King Phlegyas, bare him, a great joy to men, a soother of cruel pangs. ^v-1

**5** And so hail to you, lord: in my song I make my prayer to thee! ^v-5

# Homeric Hymn 25: To the Muses and Apollo

**1** I will begin with the Muses and Apollo and Zeus. For it is through the Muses and Apollo that there are singers upon the earth and players upon the lyre; but kings are from Zeus. Happy is he whom the Muses ^v-1

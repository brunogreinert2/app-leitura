# Homeric Hymn 23: To the Most High Son of Cronos (Zeus)

**1** I will sing of Zeus, chiefest among the gods and greatest, all-seeing, the lord of all, the fulfiller who whispers words of wisdom to Themis as she sits leaning towards him. Be gracious, all-seeing Son of Cronos, most excellent and great! ^v-1
